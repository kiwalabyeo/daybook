Your day and your money, in one place.

Daybook is a task planner and money tracker built for life in Uganda: MTN MoMo and Airtel Money payments are logged from their SMS, charges and loan fees are counted, and your tasks, calendar and focus time sit next to them.

Tasks

Quick add that understands "Call mum tomorrow 6pm #Family !!". Overdue, today, upcoming, lists.

Money

Mobile money from SMS, cash in one tap, budgets, and what charges and loans really cost you.

Calendar

Google Calendar both ways. Your tasks appear in a Daybook calendar; move one there and it moves here.

Focus

Pomodoro rounds in sets of four, live in the Dynamic Island, with widgets for the home screen.

Where it runs

iPhone (SwiftUI, with widgets and a Live Activity) and Linux (a Tauri desktop app). Both read and write the same data and stay in sync within a second. A web app and Android are planned.

Who makes it

Daybook is a personal project by Oscar Kiwalabye, an electrical engineering student at Makerere University in Kampala. It is not a company product and is used by its author and a few friends.
